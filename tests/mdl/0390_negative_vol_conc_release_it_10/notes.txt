Removal of VOLUME molecules by a negative concentration.

Only volume molecules: the one species, a, is a volume molecule
(DIFFUSION_CONSTANT_3D = 1e-6); there are no surface molecules. 100 molecules
are released into the watertight cube (0.1 um wide, 0.001 um^3) at iteration 0;
at iteration 5 the release site rel_remove, CONCENTRATION = -1e-4 (M), removes
N_AV * 1e-15 * 1e-4 * 0.001 = 60.22 molecules, rounded as on MCell3's engine:
-60.22 + 0.5 = -59.72, truncated toward zero to -59. So 41 remain. 10 iterations
of 1e-6 s, seed 1. Counted: a in the world and in the cube, every iteration.

MCell3's engine has always removed molecules for a negative number from a
concentration (vol_util.c, test_max_release stops only numbers above INT_MAX).
Until mcell step 8, MCell4 stopped such a release with "Release site
'Scene.rel_remove' tries to release more than INT_MAX (2147483647) molecules".
The counts are identical on both engines (ref_react_data_3 and _4); the
positions differ, as the engines draw differently. The suite also runs the model
through the data model and the Python API against ref_*_4, and with a
checkpoint at iteration 3 (checkpoint_iters).
