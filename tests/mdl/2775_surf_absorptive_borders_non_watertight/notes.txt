Region borders for SURFACE molecules on non-watertight and watertight meshes.

Molecules: surface molecules only. The one species, S, is a surface molecule
(DIFFUSION_CONSTANT_2D = 1e-5): it sits on the walls and diffuses within the
surface, crossing edges from wall to wall. The test has no volume molecules.
Region borders are edges of the mesh, and only surface molecules move along the
surface and cross edges. A volume molecule meets a surface class only when it
hits a wall of the region, which involves no border.

Surface class: absorb { ABSORPTIVE = S' }. A surface molecule S with
orientation ' that reaches a border of the region carrying the class is
absorbed (removed). Every S is released as S', so the rule applies to all of
them.

Six objects, side by side (x offsets 0, 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 um), all made from
the 2 x 2 x 2 um cube of 2770. On each, 1000 S' are released onto the region
that carries the class, at time 0. 500 iterations of 1e-6 s.
- The open boxes (box_*) are the cube without its top face: 10 walls. The 4
  edges of the missing face (the rim) have one wall each, so they are boundary
  edges and the mesh is non-watertight.
- The cubes (cube_*) have all 12 walls and are watertight.

Object      Class and release on            Borders of that region (MCell4)
box_all     ALL (no region of the user)     the 4 rim edges (boundary edges)
box_whole   region whole: all 10 walls      the 4 rim edges (boundary edges)
box_sides   region sides: the 8 walls of    the 4 rim edges (boundary edges) and
            the four side faces             the 4 edges of the bottom square
                                            (internal edges)
box_bottom  region bottom: the 2 walls of   the 4 edges of the bottom square
            the bottom face                 (internal edges only)
cube_all    ALL of the watertight cube      none
cube_whole  region whole: all 12 walls      none

MCell4 (ref_react_data_4) gives every region its borders by one rule, ALL
included: a wall side is a border when its edge has exactly one wall in the
region, that is, a boundary edge of the mesh or an internal edge whose other
wall is outside the region. The surface molecules on box_all and box_whole
are absorbed at the rim. Only the whole of a watertight mesh has no border.

MCell3 (ref_react_data_3) gives no borders to ALL and to a region that holds
every wall. There the surface molecules on box_all and box_whole reflect at the
rim and keep their 1000 molecules.

Counts after 500 iterations (surface molecules S on each object):
            MCell3   MCell4 (MDL and Python API)
box_all     1000     704
box_whole   1000     703
box_sides    274     258
box_bottom    71      68
cube_all    1000    1000
cube_whole  1000    1000

The molecules of all six objects share one random-number stream, so each
object's surface molecules move with other random numbers: every object is a
separate random sample.
- box_all and box_whole follow the same rule but don't have the same counts:
  704 and 703 at iteration 500, 860 and 884 at iteration 100.
- Run alone with the same seed, the class on ALL and the class on a region of
  every wall give identical counts and positions.
- Once box_all's and box_whole's molecules are absorbed on MCell4, the paths on
  the other objects shift too, so box_sides and box_bottom differ a little
  between the engines.

Outputs:
- the count of S on each object every 10 iterations (react_data, compared
  exactly);
- the positions of all molecules at iteration 500 (viz_data; the MDL tester
  requires a viz reference).

The suite also runs this model through the data model and the Python API
against ref_react_data_4, so it checks that MDL and the API give the same
result.
