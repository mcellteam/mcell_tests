Region borders for SURFACE molecules on a non-watertight mesh: one open box.

The class ABSORPTIVE = S' on ALL, but S, released: the rule doesn't match
these surface molecules, so none is absorbed on either engine, although the
rim is a border in MCell4.

Only surface molecules: the one species, S, is a surface molecule
(DIFFUSION_CONSTANT_2D = 1e-5). There are no volume molecules; region borders
are edges of the mesh and matter only for surface molecules.

The object is one open box: the 2 x 2 x 2 um cube of 2770 without its top face
(10 walls). The 4 edges of the missing face (the rim) have one wall each, so
they are boundary edges and the mesh is non-watertight. 1000 surface molecules
are released at time 0 onto the region that carries the class; 500 iterations
of 1e-6 s, seed 1.

MCell4 (ref_*_4) gives every region its borders by one rule, ALL included: a
wall side is a border when its edge has exactly one wall in the region. Here
the class's region holds every wall, so its borders are the 4 rim edges.
MCell3 (ref_*_3) gives no borders to ALL or to a region that holds every wall.

Tests 2776-2779 differ only in the region, the rule's orientation mark and the
released orientation. 2776, 2777 and 2778 store the same counts
(ref_react_data_4), and 2776 and 2777 the same positions (ref_viz_data_4). The
suite also runs each model through the data model and the Python API against
the MCell4 references.

Outputs: the count of S on the box every 10 iterations (exact), and the
positions of all molecules at iteration 500 (the MDL tester requires a viz
reference).
