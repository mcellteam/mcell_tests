TRANSPARENT for SURFACE molecules: reaction products placed across the border.

Only surface molecules: A, B and C are surface molecules
(DIFFUSION_CONSTANT_2D = 1e-6); there are no volume molecules. The class has
TRANSPARENT = A', B' and C' and is on the region 'bottom'. 1000 A' are released
there at time 0 and react A' -> B' + C' [1e5]. A product may be placed on a
tile of a neighbouring wall across the region's border, and the molecules
diffuse across it, as without the class. In MCell4, a TRANSPARENT region no
longer restricts where products go (as on MCell3, only REFLECTIVE and
ABSORPTIVE regions do), and the counts and positions are those of this model
without the surface class, molecule for molecule (checked when the test was
made). MCell3 and MCell4 differ on this model even without any surface class
(how unimolecular surface reactions run, not borders), so ref_*_3 and ref_*_4
differ.

The object is one open box: the 2 x 2 x 2 um cube of 2770 without its top face
(10 walls). The 4 edges of the missing face (the rim) have one wall each, so
they are boundary edges and the mesh is non-watertight. The region 'bottom'
holds the 2 walls of the bottom face; its border is the 4 edges of the bottom
square, internal edges shared with the side faces. 500 iterations of 1e-6 s,
seed 1.

TRANSPARENT for a surface molecule means that it passes the border of the
region, the default for surface molecules (MCell4's documentation,
libmcell/definition/constants.yaml). Until mcell step 6a, MCell4 took the
TRANSPARENT rule, which has no products, as absorptive at a border, so these
surface molecules were absorbed there. MCell3's engine has always let them pass.
